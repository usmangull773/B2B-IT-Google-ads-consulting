What I found before the call public data only

Everything below came from oxagile.com's own tracking requests in the browser, with no account access needed. It previews the kind of gaps a full audit surfaces.

Two GA4 properties are firing on every pageview

G-ESCMTZ4RPX and G-YDPYQE90RM both load and both send page_view events simultaneously. Unless this is an intentional migration, it usually means split or duplicated conversion counts feeding whoever is making bidding decisions.

No Meta Pixel detected on the homepage

Google Ads conversion tag (AW-1017527176) and GTM (GTM-KWR7G7) are both active, but no Meta pixel fired. If remarketing or cross-channel attribution matters later, that audience is not being built right now.

Consent Mode denies storage until a visitor accepts cookies

On the first pageview the consent signal reads gcs=G100, meaning ad and analytics storage are both withheld before consent. That is expected behavior, but it means a share of your conversions are modeled rather than directly observed. That share is worth checking in the account, because it changes how far to trust the reported cost per acquisition.

GTM container is correctly structured

Tags run through one GTM container (GTM-KWR7G7), so the fixes above are configuration changes rather than a rebuild. That keeps the work low-risk to your live tracking.

Why this matters for the ask: your post asks for feedback on account setup and a prioritized list of recommendations. If the data feeding your specialist's bidding decisions is duplicated across two GA4 properties or partly modeled, every optimization built on top of it inherits that noise. Confirming the measurement layer is clean is usually the first move, before touching bids or keywords.

Who the campaigns need to reach

Oxagile sells outsourced software development to enterprise buyers, so the targeting logic should follow from that rather than a generic B2B template.

Buyer profile

CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and founders at mid to large tech, media, and telecom companies evaluating outsourced dev partners. Long sales cycles, high deal value, low monthly search volume on the terms that matter.

What that changes

Lead quality matters more than lead volume. Branded terms, high-intent long-tail search phrases, and tight negative keyword lists usually outperform broad reach for this buyer.
